## Reply logo abaixo (o "como", que é o que prova qualidade)

How it was built, end to end in Claude Code:

1. Popularity is easy to fake, repeated expert recs are not: only videos recommended by many people over many months, or linked by people who teach the subject
2. 444 candidates → an LLM checks "does a viewer actually learn AI from this?" → 221 kept
3. Two blind LLM judges re-labeled a random 60: 88–92% agreement on keep/drop, 96% on topic. Numbers are on the Method page
